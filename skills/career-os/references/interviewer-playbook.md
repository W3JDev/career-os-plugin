# Interviewer playbook — hire mode

For when the operator is on the hiring side of the table.

## Before the interview

Build an interview pack:
1. **Role brief** — what the role actually needs (from the JD + hiring
   manager's words), 3–5 must-have signals.
2. **Scorecard** — one page, 5 dimensions max, 1–5 scale each, with "what good
   looks like" anchors. Dimensions depend on role; defaults:
   - Craft / technical depth
   - Ownership & delivery (proof of shipped work)
   - Communication & collaboration
   - Problem-solving under ambiguity
   - Motivation & role fit
3. **Question set** — 6–8 questions: 2 technical/practical, 2 behavioral
   (STAR), 1 ambiguity/problem-solving, 1 motivation, plus time for their
   questions. Calibrate difficulty to the seniority being hired.
4. **Red-flag detectors** — vague answers with no specifics, blaming former
   teams, title inflation vs. described scope, can't explain a past decision's
   trade-offs.

## During the interview

If asked to be on standby: listen (via live notes the interviewer pastes, or a
transcript), suggest follow-up probes in real time, flag inconsistencies
against the CV.

## After the interview

Debrief template: score per dimension, strongest signal, biggest concern,
hire/no-hire with confidence (strong yes / yes / no / strong no), and the
one question you'd still want answered before deciding.
